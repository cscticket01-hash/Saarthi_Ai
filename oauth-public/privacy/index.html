Vidya Saarthi Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Vidya Saarthi is a school-management application. This policy explains how information is handled when a school uses Vidya Saarthi and connects a Google account.

Information we use

When a school chooses to connect Google, Vidya Saarthi may receive basic Google account identity information such as the account email address and authorization needed for app-created Google Drive files.

Google Drive access

Vidya Saarthi requests access only to files and folders that the app creates or that the user explicitly uses with the app. The connection is used to create and manage school backups and related school files in the connected school's Google Drive.

School data

School records are associated with the relevant school account and are designed to remain isolated from other schools. Existing school data is not intentionally overwritten merely by connecting Google Drive.

How information is used

Information is used to provide school-management features, authenticate connections, maintain school data, and create or restore app-managed backups. We do not use Google user data for advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell Google user data. Data may be processed by service providers needed to operate the application and its cloud functionality, subject to their applicable terms and safeguards.

Security

We use reasonable technical controls intended to protect account connections and keep school data separated. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Google access is optional. A user can revoke Vidya Saarthi's Google access from their Google Account permissions. Revoking access can prevent Google Drive backup features from working.

Contact
