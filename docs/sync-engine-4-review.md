## Stage 5: interrupted record handoff recovery

The existing provider receipt poll can also restore that marker before accepting a final revision. Recovery is limited to the original same-school eligible pending/retry record, and rejects other operation IDs, foreign tickets, atomic/dependency-owned operations or unapproved receipts. UI diagnostic evidence checks the actual record/photo ticket digest rather than only a hash-shaped string. Canonical record completion rejects temporary live/Cloudflare revision prefixes. Added source regressions for restart recovery and wrong-ticket rejection. Historical journals without the explicit authorization marker retain their prior safe retry path; file/document and superseded-operation recovery are not covered by this change. No runtime or production verification performed in this stage.

## Stage 6: same-record staged-write ordering

For calendar/notice record writes, the existing serialized local queue now retains a known Cloudflare-owned predecessor when the user saves a newer record version, including a save arriving while the old stage response is in flight. The newer operation stays durable and is excluded from the normal send, batch, and automatic repair paths until the existing provider-receipt owner verifies the predecessor's exact ticket and canonical revision. Settlement uses the existing outbox acknowledgement transaction; it retains the newer operation, refreshes its CAS base, and invalidates stale prepared wire data. A nested deletion depending on the same predecessor retains its ordered revision. Test source covers later edits and an in-flight local save. This is not a generalized person/financial/document predecessor implementation or runtime proof. No production deployment or manually dispatched workflow.

## Stage 13: self-repair of missing local Drive delivery receipt journals

An original record, optimized portrait or document may have already persisted a verified Cloudflare staging CAS, while its *read-only local polling journal* was lost or was never created after a crash. The existing Drive delivery receipt owner now recovers such a journal from the original immutable same-school ticket, after an authenticated `managed/cloudflare/operation` status read of that exact lane/id. The verifier independently recomputes the record or sidecar ticket and, for an original portrait, rechecks the cached raw media digest. Document outboxes without a `collection` field are still supported by their exact original document ID and generation. Only a pending or verified provider status for that same ticket may recreate journal metadata; reconstruction never uploads bytes, resubmits a business mutation, clears a provider review row or fabricates a Drive acknowledgement. A provider-already-verified replay is retained as `pending` *local CAS settlement* until the existing poll verifies its exact canonical record/file receipt. Foreign, malformed or missing statuses leave the original durable outbox unchanged.

The maintenance scan is limited to at most three authenticated missing-ticket probes and at most one scan per active profile/school per minute. Healthy pending or review-only journal entries are excluded before original portrait digest checks, and ordinary receipt polling still uses its existing cadence. Source regression cases cover matching and foreign record tickets, document generation binding, already-verified record/document reconstruction, incorrect provider tickets and preservation of explicit review-only receipts. This is source code, not runtime/provider evidence.
